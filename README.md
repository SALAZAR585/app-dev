# My Favorite Anime: Jujutsu Kaisen

> "Throughout Heaven and Earth, I alone am the honored one." – Satoru Gojo

## Overview
**Jujutsu Kaisen** is a dark fantasy/supernatural series following *Yuji Itadori*, a high school student who joins a secret organization of Jujutsu Sorcerers after swallowing a powerful cursed object to protect his friends.

### Main Characters
1. **Yuji Itadori** - *Host of Sukuna & Physical Powerhouse*
2. **Megumi Fushiguro** - *Ten Shadows Technique user*
3. **Nobara Kugisaki** - *Straw Doll Technique user*
4. **Satoru Gojo** - *The Strongest Jujutsu Sorcerer*

### Key Features of the Series
* **Dynamic Animation:** High-energy combat sequences animated by MAPPA.
* **Complex Magic System:** Deep world-building centered around Cursed Energy and Domain Expansions.
* **Compelling Antagonists:** Formidable villains with distinct philosophies.

## Why I Like It
* Fast-paced action scenes with top-tier choreography
* Unpredictable plot developments and high stakes
* Great dynamic and banter between the main trio
* Visually impressive Domain Expansion sequences

## Series Information
* **Genre:** Action / Supernatural / Dark Fantasy
* **Release Year:** 2020
* **Studio:** MAPPA
